Give

Generosity that fuels the mission

Thank you for partnering with us. Your giving helps the Gospel spread by word and deed.

Everything we have is a gift from God. Giving is a joyful act of worship and trust — and it fuels the mission of the Gospel in our city and beyond.

Ways to Give

Simple, secure, and flexible

Online

Give a one-time or recurring gift securely from any device.

In Person

Drop a gift in the box at any of our Sunday gatherings.

By Mail

Send a check to 100 Grove Avenue, Springfield, IL 62704.

Text to Give

Text GIVE to (555) 123-4567 and follow the prompts.
