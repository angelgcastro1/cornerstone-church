Our Ministries

A place for every season of life

Discover where you and your family can grow, belong, and join God's mission.

From our youngest kids to the far corners of the globe, our ministries exist to help people take their next step in following Jesus.

Cornerstone Kids

Where kids meet Jesus

Every Sunday, newborns through 5th grade experience safe, joyful, age-appropriate environments where they learn that God loves them and His Word is true.

Learn about Kids

Youth Ministry

Middle & high school

Our student ministry gathers weekly for worship, teaching, small groups, and a whole lot of fun — helping teenagers build a faith that lasts.

Learn about Youth

Connection Groups

Life is better together

Sunday is a great start, but real community happens in smaller circles. Our groups meet throughout the week in homes across the city to share life and grow together.

Find a group

The Salt Company

College ministry

A thriving community of college students pursuing Jesus together — through large-group worship, small groups, and everyday friendship on campus.

Learn about College

Local Missions

Loving our city

We partner with schools, shelters, and neighbors across Springfield to meet real needs and share the hope of the Gospel right where we live.

Get involved locally

Global Missions

Reaching the nations

We send, support, and pray for partners around the world who are bringing the good news of Jesus to places it has never been heard.
