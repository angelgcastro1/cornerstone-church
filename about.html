Who We Are

A people shaped by the Gospel

We are an ordinary church of ordinary people who have been changed by an extraordinary God — learning together what it means to follow Jesus in everyday life.

Our Story

From a living room to a citywide family

Cornerstone Church began in 2004 as a handful of families praying in a living room, longing to see the Gospel take root in Springfield. What started small has grown into a diverse community gathering each Sunday across our city.

Through every season, our conviction has stayed the same: the good news of Jesus changes everything. We're committed to that message — in word and in deed — until Christ returns.

What We Believe

The truths at our foundation

Our beliefs are historic, biblical, and shared with the global church across centuries. Here's a summary of the convictions that anchor us.

We believe the Bible is the inspired, authoritative Word of God — sufficient and trustworthy for all of life, faith, and practice.
We believe in one God, eternally existing in three persons — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit — perfect in holiness, wisdom, and love.
We believe salvation is by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone. Jesus lived, died, and rose again to reconcile us to God.
We believe the church is God's family and mission — a community sent to make disciples of all nations for the glory of God.

Mission & Values

Glorify God & make disciples

Everything we do flows from a single mission and the values that shape how we pursue it together.

01

Gospel-Centered

We keep the good news of Jesus at the center of our worship, relationships, and mission — never assuming it, always returning to it.

02

Disciple-Making

We help one another follow Jesus and equip every person to help someone else do the same.

03

Life Together

We were made for community. We pursue authentic relationships where we're known, loved, and challenged to grow.

04

On Mission

We love our city and the nations, giving ourselves to see the Gospel spread by word and deed everywhere we go.
