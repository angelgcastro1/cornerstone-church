Sundays at Cornerstone

What to expect on your first visit

Our gatherings are warm, unhurried, and centered on Jesus. You'll find heartfelt worship, teaching from the Bible, and people genuinely glad you came. Come as you are — no dress code, no pressure.

Not sure where to go? Look for a host in the lobby wearing a name tag. They'll help you find everything from coffee to kids check-in.
